Plan

Every city lines up.

Name a meeting, an alarm or a goal and pick a time. TimePeace shows what that time is in your other cities.

Alarms

It waits for you.

An alarm pops up on the watch, in front of everything, until you press Dismiss or Snooze.

Sticky notes

Notes that stay put.

Paper notes on your desktop, each with a curled corner and a pin. Pick a color or a paper, then pin it so it stays where you left it.

Settings

Everything in plain words.

A side rail lists Look, Clocks, Notes, Desktop, Shortcuts, Voice typing and About. Pick one, or search for what you want and go straight to it.

18 animated layouts

Little scenes that move behind your clocks. Your cities sit right in them.

Not a fan of motion? The Animate layout switch in Settings freezes any scene into a still picture.

Up and running

  1. Download

    One file, TimePeace.exe.

  2. Open it

    If Windows shows a blue SmartScreen box, choose More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Make it yours

    Add your cities, pick a look, and drag the clock where you want it.

Is it safe?

  • Windows warns because TimePeace is not code signed yet.
  • No account, no telemetry, no analytics.
  • It goes online only to check GitHub for updates once a day, and once to download the speech model if you use dictation.
  • Updates are checked against the checksum published with each release.
