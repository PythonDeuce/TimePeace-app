Version 1.12.0 · 2026-11-09

What's new

Changed

  • Every clock template is easier to read. Times are bigger and use even-width digits, and city names and dates are larger. Nothing is shrunk to fit any more, so the small watch size reads as clearly as the large one.
  • Soft sheet, Daylight bars and Split panel were rebuilt to use the whole face, with each city on one line and its time beside it.
  • Three new scenic layouts replace Cards, Dial and Typographic: Paper Lanterns, Kite Festival and Glasshouse. If you had one of the old layouts picked, TimePeace switches you to its replacement and keeps your placement.

Fixed

  • The local city label on Split panel is no longer cut off at the small size, and AM/PM no longer runs past the edge of the face.
